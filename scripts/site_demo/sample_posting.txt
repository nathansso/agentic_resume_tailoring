Data Science Intern, Growth Analytics, Summer 2027
Brightwell Market (sample posting written for this demo)
Hybrid, 12 weeks

About the internship
Brightwell Market is an online grocery marketplace. You will join the growth analytics team and help it decide which product changes to ship. We want an intern who can run experiments, find out why customers leave, and explain what the data says to people who do not work with data every day.

What you'll do
- Design and analyze A/B tests of checkout and sign-up changes.
- Build models that predict which subscribers are likely to cancel.
- Turn raw order data into dashboards the product team checks every week.
- Write up results for product managers and designers in plain language.

Required qualifications
- Currently pursuing a B.S. or M.S. in computer science, statistics, data science or a related field.
- Experience with SQL and Python, including pandas.
- Experience designing or analyzing experiments, for example A/B tests and significance testing.
- Familiarity with machine learning libraries such as scikit-learn.
- Ability to explain results clearly to a non-technical audience.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience building dashboards, for example with Streamlit or Tableau.
