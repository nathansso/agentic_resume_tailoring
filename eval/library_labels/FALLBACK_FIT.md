## How far to trust these figures

- The vocabulary was written with both labelled sets in view, so the fitted rows are a fit and the leave-one-out row tests only the three numbers.
- The holdout was written before the vocabulary, but by the same author, who then added kinds of work (`dba`, `sales`) after writing it. It shows the vocabulary's reach into fresh wording; it is not an independent test. Its labels are unreviewed.
- A bullet that repeats the posting's nouns while describing teaching or office work scores 0 (`concepts.mostly_unasked`); a keyword-stuffed bullet the vocabulary cannot place is the case this fallback cannot see.
- The pick reads requirements. A job with none gets `no_match` for variants, and the track lookup of #229 (the title's role family) for tracks.
